Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is the "Winter Arc"? +
The Winter Arc is a seasonal mental model where you commit to 90 continuous days of disciplined self-improvement throughout late autumn and winter. While others slow down during the holidays, you cultivate intense focus across fitness, intellect, and career.
Is it really 100% free with no hidden charges? +
Yes, completely. There are no premium tiers, no recurring subscriptions, and no upsells. It is built as an open-source project by and for individuals striving for personal mastery.
Where is my personal tracking data stored? +
All checkbox logs, weight trends, and DSA progress are saved directly into your web browser's localStorage. Flipbook photos are stored inside IndexedDB. Nothing ever gets transmitted to any server. You can export a JSON backup at any time.
How do I install it on my phone? +
Open https://nimratsingh217.github.io/winter-arc/ on your mobile browser. On iOS Safari, tap the Share icon and select "Add to Home Screen". On Android Chrome, tap the three dots and select "Install App". It will launch full-screen just like a native app.
What happens if I miss a single day? +
The core maxim of the Winter Arc is: "Consistency over perfection. Miss a day, never miss twice." Do not restart from Day 1. Accept the imperfect day, log it honestly, and lock back in the very next morning.
