Design Philosophy

Why Winter Arc Is Different

A tool built for personal consistency, free from modern software bloat.

Free to Use

All features are completely open and accessible to everyone. No payment walls, trial periods, or feature restrictions.

No Account or Sign-In

Start immediately without creating an account, confirming emails, or providing personal credentials. Just open and begin.

Your Data Stays on Your Device

Logs and images are stored strictly in your browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). Includes one-click JSON backup export and import.

A Real Finish Line

Unlike endless habit apps, Winter Arc has a defined 90-day timeline divided into 3 structured phases to help you finish what you start.

The 90-Day Journey

Three Phases of Progression

Your next 90 days are split into three 30-day blocks, moving from habit formation to high consistency.

Phase 01

Foundation

Day 1 → Day 30

Build the routine. Focus on showing up every single day, establishing hydration and nutrition baselines, and getting your workouts in order.

Phase 02

Build

Day 31 → Day 60

Raise consistency. Protect your streaks, steady your training volume, stay regular with daily study, and eliminate unneeded friction.

Phase 03

Peak

Day 61 → Day 90

Finish strong. Maintain your standard through the final stretch, defeat your weekly bosses, and reach the summit on Day 90.

Habit Structure

The 5 Core Pillars

Twelve daily habits grouped into five practical areas of daily discipline.

Nutrition

Breakfast, lunch, pre-workout timing, hitting your protein target (100g+), and daily hydration (2.5–3L).

Gym & Rest

Physical training 4–5 times a week. Tap for gym completed, or hold to log a scheduled rest day.

Skin & Hygiene

Morning face care, post-workout cleanse, and consistent nightly tooth brushing without skipping.

Mind & Discipline

A personal self-discipline habit you tick each night. Use it if it matters to you.

Skills & DSA

One productive career action daily plus dedicated DSA problem solving with the built-in timer.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Winter Arc? +
Winter Arc is a 90-day personal transformation challenge designed to help you build discipline, consistency, and better daily habits. You can start the challenge on any day you choose—Day 1 begins whenever you are ready.
Is Winter Arc completely free? +
Yes, it is completely free to use. There are no paid tiers, subscriptions, or hidden charges.
Where is my tracking data saved? +
All habit ticks, streaks, and timer records are stored directly in your browser's local storage. Flipbook photos are saved in on-device IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to a remote server, and you can export a JSON backup at any time.
How do I install the tracker as an app? +
On Google Chrome desktop, click the Install button on the right side of the address bar. On iOS Safari, tap the Share icon and select "Add to Home Screen". On Android Chrome, tap the three dots and select "Install app".
What if I miss a day during the 90 days? +
The core principle is consistency over perfection: miss a day, never miss twice. If you miss a day, keep moving forward without restarting from Day 1.

How to Install Winter Arc

Winter Arc is an installable web app that runs directly on your device.

Google Chrome (Desktop) Click the Install icon on the right side of the URL address bar.
iPhone & iPad (Safari) Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen, scroll down, and select Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome) Tap the three-dot menu icon in the top right and select Install app or Add to Home screen.
